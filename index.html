6-AXIS IMU 16 FEATURES EDGE PROCESSING MOVEMENT ANALYSIS

01 · The ProblemWhy watch movement continuously?

Parkinson’s patients can experience autonomic dysfunction leading to orthostatic hypotension (a drop in blood pressure on standing) and fainting. Beyond those events, movement changes shape daily life: gait abnormalities, movement instability, tremor-related oscillations, freezing-related gait changes, and potentially dangerous movement events.

These changes appear in short windows and vary hour to hour — capturing them continuously needs a sensor that lives on the body.

02 · The SolutionWear it where movement happens

Parkin Pulse is a lightweight ankle-worn IMU device for continuous movement monitoring. The ankle is involved directly in gait and lower-limb motion, giving useful access to walking rhythm, step timing, stride variability, movement intensity, and frequency-domain gait characteristics.

Walking rhythm

Step timing and cadence sensed at their source.

Stride variability

Cycle-to-cycle timing consistency.

Movement intensity

Lower-limb acceleration across a window.

Spectral content

Frequency-domain gait / tremor characteristics.

03 · HardwareThe device

ESP32-S3

Embedded controller that acquires and processes sensor data on-device.

MPU6050

6-axis IMU — 3-axis accelerometer (ax, ay, az) + 3-axis gyroscope (gx, gy, gz).

Temperature Probe

Auxiliary temperature measurement (not presented as a validated biomarker).

3D Printed Case

Custom enclosure for the wearable electronics.

9V Battery

Prototype power source.

LM2596 Converter

Power regulation / conversion stage.

MPU6050 → ESP32-S3 → Signal Processing → Feature Extraction → Analysis → Companion Interface

05 · The 16 FeaturesTime, frequency & gait descriptors

01 Mean Accel Magnitude

|a| = √(ax²+ay²+az²)

Average movement magnitude.

02 Accel Std Dev

Acceleration variability.

03 RMS

Root-mean-square magnitude / energy.

04 Peak-to-Peak

max(a) − min(a).

05 Signal Magnitude Area

Aggregated absolute acceleration.

06 Zero-Crossing Rate

Crossings around the mean level.

07 Skewness

Distribution asymmetry.

08 Kurtosis

Peakedness / heavy tails.

09 Dominant Frequency

Strongest spectral component.

10 Tremor Band Power

Power in ~4–6 Hz relative to total.

11 Freeze Index

P(3–8 Hz) / P(0.5–3 Hz)

Frequency-domain FOG-research ratio.

12 Spectral Entropy

Concentration of spectral power.

13 Mean Stride Time

Average inter-step interval.

14 Stride-Time CV%

CV = σ/mean × 100

Gait timing variability.

15 Cadence

Steps per minute.

16 Step Regularity

Autocorrelation-based gait consistency.
